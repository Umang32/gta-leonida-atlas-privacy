Privacy Policy GTA Leonida Atlas · RAM Technology Effective date: November 21, 2026 RAM Technology develops GTA Leonida Atlas, an unofficial Android exploration companion. This policy explains how the app handles information and how to contact us. Information stored on your device The app stores your checklist progress, private notes, demo progress, downloaded location records, and the last successful sync time in its app-private storage. Notes and checklist progress are not uploaded to RAM Technology or included in location-data requests. The app does not require an account. Location-data requests and third parties The app contacts FullSweep over HTTPS to download information about locations in the game. It attempts a sync when opened and when you choose to sync. These requests necessarily expose your IP address and connection information to FullSweep and its hosting or network providers, which may process or retain this information under their own practices. We do not control their server logs. See FullSweep's privacy policy. The app does not request your real-world GPS location. References to locations and coordinates describe the game world, not your physical position. Advertising, analytics, and accounts The current app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs, game-account login, cloud synchronization, or in-app purchases. RAM Technology does not operate an app backend that receives your notes or checklist progress. Support messages If you contact dev@ksumiyo.com, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to answer your request and investigate reported issues. Our email service providers process these communications to deliver and store them. Do not send passwords, signing keys, or other sensitive information. Sharing We do not sell your personal information. Information sent to third-party services as described above is handled by those services. Support communications may be disclosed where required by applicable law or as necessary to address the support request. Retention and deletion App data remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. You can delete local data through Android Settings → Apps → GTA Leonida Atlas → Storage → Clear storage; labels may differ by device. This deletes saved notes and progress. Deleting local app data does not delete third-party server logs or email correspondence. We retain support correspondence as needed to resolve and follow up on your request, and to meet applicable obligations. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence; some records may need to be retained to meet applicable obligations. Security The app uses Android app-private storage for local records and HTTPS for location-data requests. No system can guarantee absolute security. Avoid storing sensitive personal information in notes. Children The app is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through support communications, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action. Policy updates We may update this policy as the app changes. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Material changes will also be communicated in the app when appropriate.
